Why this version matters

Built to help template buyers feel the jump from basic storefront to premium polish.

The Professional package is designed for sellers who need more than a homepage and a product list. It adds the pages buyers expect when judging whether a template feels complete: profile, contact, about, error handling, and now a proper auth flow.

That extra coverage makes it easier to position the product for fashion stores, electronics shops, home catalogs, digital retail demos, and any marketplace listing that benefits from stronger perceived value.
